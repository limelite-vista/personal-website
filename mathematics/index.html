current research

Hausdorff dimension of flag limit sets

Ongoing project · supervised by Wenyu Pan

I am working on the Hausdorff dimension of limit sets of Schottky subgroups of SL3(ℝ) acting on the full flag variety. The aim is to establish a dimension formula in this setting, bringing together fractal geometry, discrete groups and dynamics.

A full flag in ℝ3 is a line contained in a plane, both through the origin. Studying limit sets of these flags connects the geometry of the group action with questions about fractal dimension.
